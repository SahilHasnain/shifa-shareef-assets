Worship And Rituals

  • Dua (دعا): Supplication; personal prayer to Allah.
  • Wudu (وضو): Ritual ablution performed with water before prayer.
  • Ghusl (غسل): Full ritual bath required after certain impurities.
  • Sajdah (سجدة): Prostration; the act of placing one's forehead on the ground in worship.
  • Rak'ah (ركعة): A unit of the Islamic prayer (salah), consisting of standing, bowing, prostrating, and sitting.
  • Adhan (اذان): The call to prayer. A mu'adhdhin is the one who calls the adhan.
  • Kalimah (كلمة): The declaration of faith: "There is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."
  • Qiblah (قبلة): The direction of prayer facing the Sacred Mosque in Makkah.
  • Tafsir (تفسير): Exegesis or commentary on the Qur'an.
  • Bay'ah (بيعة): Pledge of allegiance, especially to the Prophet ﷺ or to a rightful leader.
