Seventh Section: The Qur'an's Inimitability By Rendering A People Unable

The four categories mentioned above are manifest in proving that the Noble Qur'an is a miracle, and there is no doubt in them. Besides these, there are also verses that were revealed to render a people unable in a particular matter. For example:

"Say: if the final home with Allah is purely for you..." (al-Baqarah: 94)

Regarding the Jews, Allah عز وجل said: "Then wish for death." (al-Baqarah: 94)

The Qur'an informed them that they would never be able to wish for death, and that is exactly what happened.
