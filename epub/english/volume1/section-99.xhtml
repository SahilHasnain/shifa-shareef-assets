Which Rights Of Huzoor Are Obligatory Upon The Ummah?

Qadi Iyad Maliki رحمة الله تعالى عليه says that he divided this Second Part into four chapters. The summary of all of them is that it is obligatory to affirm Huzoor عليه السلام, to follow and obey his Sunnah, to love him and be sincere toward him, to honor and revere him, to behave well with him, to send durood upon him, and to visit his luminous grave. These are necessary upon every Muslim.
