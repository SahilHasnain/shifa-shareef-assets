Fourth Section: The Inheritance, Washing, And Funeral Prayer Of One Who Insults The Prophet

Regarding a person who is killed for having insulted Huzoor عليه السلام, the statements concerning his washing and funeral prayer are as follows:

The scholars differ concerning his inheritance. The school of Sahnoon رحمة الله تعالى عليه is that it goes to the Muslim community. Asbagh رحمة الله تعالى عليه said that his inheritance goes to his Muslim heirs if he used to conceal his statement. If he used to say it openly, then it is the right of the Muslims, meaning it will be deposited in the public treasury. In any case, he will be killed and his repentance will not be accepted.

There are many statements of the scholars in this. Abul Hasan Qalibi رحمة الله تعالى عليه said that if he was killed because he denied testimony against him, then his inheritance is ruled according to what appears from his confession, meaning his heirs are entitled to his inheritance. The ruling of killing is the hadd for the matter established against him; it has no connection with inheritance.

Similarly, if he confesses to the abuse and displays repentance, he will certainly be killed, for that is his hadd, but his inheritance and all other rulings will be according to Islam.

And if he confesses to the abuse and also persists upon it and refuses to repent, then he is killed. He will be a kafir. His inheritance belongs to the Muslims. He will not be given a bath, nor will janazah prayer be performed over him, nor will he be given a shroud. Rather, he will be wrapped in his clothes and buried in a pit, in the same way that disbelievers are buried.

The statement of Shaykh Abul Hasan رحمة الله تعالى عليه regarding the person who openly abuses Huzoor عليه السلام and also persists in it is clear. In this, the possibility of disagreement does not arise, because he is a kafir and apostate who neither repented nor desisted. This statement agrees with the statement of Asbagh رحمة الله تعالى عليه. Similarly, it is in the book of Sahnoon رحمة الله تعالى عليه concerning the zindiq who persists in his statement. Likewise is the statement of Ibn al-Qasim رحمة الله تعالى عليه and a group of the companions of Imam Malik رحمة الله تعالى عليه concerning the one who openly declares his kufr. Ibn al-Qasim رحمة الله تعالى عليه said that his ruling is the ruling of an apostate: neither his Muslim heirs inherit from him, nor does he inherit from those into whose religion he has entered. His bequests are not valid, nor is freeing his slaves permissible. This is also the statement of Asbagh رحمة الله تعالى عليه. According to the statement of Imam Malik رحمة الله تعالى عليه, the inheritance of an apostate goes to the Muslim community. Many other statements have also been explained in this regard.
