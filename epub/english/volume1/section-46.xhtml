Twentieth Section: The Justice, Trustworthiness, Chastity, And Truthfulness Of Huzoor

Huzoor عليه السلام was the most trustworthy of all people, the greatest in justice, and the most truthful among people. These were such qualities that even his harshest enemies acknowledged them. Huzoor عليه السلام was called al-Amin because Allah عز وجل had gathered righteous manners in him.

When Quraysh differed regarding the placement of the Black Stone, then upon seeing Huzoor عليه السلام, they said: "This is al-Amin; we are pleased with his judgment." Even in the age of ignorance, people would come to Huzoor عليه السلام for judgment in their disputes.

Huzoor عليه السلام said: "I am trustworthy on earth and in the heavens."

Even the enemies acknowledged that he was truthful, saying that they did not consider him a liar, but they denied what he had brought.

Huzoor عليه السلام never touched any woman who was not in his lawful possession, meaning neither his wife nor his bondwoman.
