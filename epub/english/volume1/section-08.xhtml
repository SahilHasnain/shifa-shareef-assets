The Rank Of Ash-Shifa

After receiving acceptance in the court of Prophethood, this book attained a special status in the eyes of scholars and the righteous in every age. They praised it in both poetry and prose.

To this day, approximately twenty-six commentaries and summaries of this book have been written. Among them, Sharh Mulla Ali Qari and Naseem ar-Riyad by Hazrat Allama Ahmad Shahabuddin Khafaji رحمة الله تعالى عليه are especially well-known and accepted.
