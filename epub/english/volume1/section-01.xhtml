About The Author

Name: Iyad ibn Musa ibn Iyad al-Maliki رحمة الله تعالى عليه.

Kunyah: Abul Fadl.

Year of birth: 476 AH, corresponding to 1083 CE.

Place of birth: Ceuta.

Forefathers: The ancestors of the honorable author رحمة الله تعالى عليه were from Andalus. His late grandfather migrated from there to Fez, and then from Fez he moved to Ceuta.
