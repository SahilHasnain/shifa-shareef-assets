Twenty-Sixth Section: On The Meanings Of Difficult Words In The Hadith

In this final section of the chapter, the author of Kitab ash-Shifa explained the rare and difficult words found in the hadith. Since those meanings have already passed within the translation, the translator considered it unnecessary to repeat them here and therefore left them out.

Chapter Three: The Rank And Worth Of Huzoor In The Light Of Hadith

This chapter mentions those sound hadith in which the immense rank and worth of Huzoor عليه السلام in the court of Allah عز وجل is described, along with those honored distinctions which he possesses in both worlds.
