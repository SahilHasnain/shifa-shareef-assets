Twenty-Seventh Section: Proofs And Signs Of Prophethood And Messengerhood

Among the proofs of the Prophethood of Huzoor عليه السلام and the signs of his Messengerhood are those hadith narrated from monks, rabbis, and scholars of the People of the Book regarding the praise of Huzoor عليه السلام and his Ummah, his name and signs, and the Seal of Prophethood that was between his two blessed shoulders.

In this matter there are reports from the earlier people of tawhid. The Torah and Injil contained descriptions and qualities of Huzoor عليه السلام. After accepting Islam, several people narrated and confirmed these descriptions, such as Hazrat Abdullah ibn Salam, Hazrat Ka'b, and others who were Jewish scholars and then became Muslim.

Huzoor عليه السلام repeatedly challenged the Jews, but the Jews altered the Torah, concealed the praise of Huzoor عليه السلام, and remained far from the truth.
