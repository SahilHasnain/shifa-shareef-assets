The Acceptance Of Ash-Shifa In The Court Of Prophethood

Imam adh-Dhahabi رحمة الله تعالى عليه mentions in Tadhkirat al-Huffaz that once, in a dream, the nephew of Qadi Iyad saw him seated with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ upon a throne of gold. Seeing this scene, awe overcame him.

Qadi Iyad said, "O my nephew, hold firmly to Kitab ash-Shifa and make it your guide on the path."

It was as though he indicated that the rank being witnessed was granted due to the writing of Ash-Shifa.
