The Urdu Translator

This English edition was prepared from the Roman Urdu edition, which itself was based on Urdu. The Urdu translation before us was that of Allama Ghulam Muinuddin رحمة الله تعالى عليه. Brief details about him are recorded below.

Birth: 23 December 1923, corresponding to 10 Rabi al-Akhir 1342 AH.

Place of birth: Moradabad, India.

Name: Ghulam Muinuddin.

Father's name: Sabirullah Shah Chishti Sabiri Ashrafi Naeemi.

Family: A Sayyid family.

Bay'ah: He رحمة الله تعالى عليه gave bay'ah at the blessed hand of Maulana Sayyid Naeemuddin Muradabadi رحمة الله تعالى عليه.
